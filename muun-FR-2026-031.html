Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of what we'll be supplying, installing, and commissioning at Muun. The package is grouped into five areas: kitchen extraction with Reco-Air, fire suppression, air conditioning, supply and extract ventilation, and commissioning and handover.

Kitchen Extraction with Reco-Air Recirculation

  • Reco-Air recirculation unit (matched to specification) A factory-built Reco-Air recirculation unit handling extract from the kitchen canopy. Multi-stage filtration including grease, carbon, and electrostatic stages cleans the air to a standard that allows it to be returned to the building rather than discharged externally. The right choice for sites without a viable external flue route, and a planning-friendly solution in sensitive mixed-use locations.
  • Stainless-steel kitchen extraction canopy A stainless-steel canopy hood positioned over the cooking line, sized to suit the equipment beneath. Captures grease, smoke, steam, and combustion gases at source. Supplied complete with grease filters and lighting strip.
  • Galvanised ductwork and all fixings All extract duct runs in galvanised mild steel between the canopy and the Reco-Air unit, fabricated and installed to BESA DW 144 standard. Includes every bracket, support, anti-vibration mount, flexible connector, and fixing needed to install the system properly.

Fire Suppression — Ansul Double-Tank System

  • Ansul R-102 wet-chemical fire suppression — double-tank An Ansul R-102 wet-chemical system installed under the kitchen extraction canopy, designed to detect and suppress cooking-equipment fires automatically. Double-tank configuration to provide the agent volume required for the cooking line. Industry standard for insurance compliance and code requirements in commercial kitchens.
  • Detection, nozzle layout, and gas/electric shut-off interlock Heat-detection links positioned over each cooking appliance, suppression nozzles aimed per the Ansul design, and a manual pull station at the kitchen exit. The system interlocks with the gas/electric supply so that on activation, the energy source to the cooking line is automatically shut off.
  • Commissioning and certification System commissioned and certified by an Ansul-trained engineer. Certification documentation issued on handover for your insurance and building-control records. Annual service interval recommended thereafter.

Air Conditioning — Ground Floor & Basement

  • Ground floor — 5 kW wall-mounted unit and 12.5 kW ceiling cassette Two-unit arrangement covering the ground floor: a 5 kW wall-mounted split for one zone and a 12.5 kW four-way cassette for the main area. Sized to deliver year-round comfort cooling and heating across the floor, with discreet positioning to suit the customer space.
  • Basement — 12.5 kW ceiling cassette A 12.5 kW four-way ceiling cassette serving the basement, matched to the floor area and occupancy. Cool, heat, and dehumidify on demand from a wireless or wall-mounted controller.
  • Refrigerant pipework, condensate, and electrical containment All refrigerant pipework, insulation, condensate drainage, and electrical containment installed to F-Gas standard. Indoor units paired to appropriately sized outdoor condensers. Commissioning and F-Gas registration of the new refrigerant circuits included.

Supply Air & Ancillary Extract

  • Basement supply air with battery heater A complete supply-air arrangement for the basement: supply fan, filter bag housing, electric battery heater to temper the incoming air during cold months, and all associated ductwork. Sized to balance the basement against extract and keep the space comfortable and pressure-neutral.
  • Ground floor rear store room — supply fan and filter bag A dedicated supply fan with filter bag housing serving the rear store room on the ground floor. Brings fresh, filtered air into the space and supports the wider building balance.
  • Basement toilet extract — fan serving two WCs A single dedicated extract fan serving both basement toilets, sized to comply with Building Regs Part F minimum extract rates. Continuous or run-on operation with appropriate controls.
  • Ground floor extract — fan with fire damper An extract fan serving the ground floor with an integrated fire damper on the duct penetration, in accordance with the building's fire strategy. Properly fixed, commissioned, and labelled for inspection.

Commissioning & Handover

  • Full system commissioning and airflow balancing Once installation is complete, we run all systems up, verify supply and extract performance against design, and balance airflows so the kitchen, WCs, ground floor, and basement are all properly ventilated and pressure-balanced. Reco-Air unit, AC units, and battery heater verified through their full operating range.
  • BESA-compliant installation documentation Installation carried out under our BESA certification (HV020676) to DW 144 / DW 172 standards. Commissioning certificates issued for the ventilation system, the AC systems (F-Gas registered), and the Ansul system on handover.
  • Product data sheets and client walkthrough on handover Technical data sheets for all installed equipment provided on the day. We walk you through the systems — how to operate them, how to change filters, what to check day to day, and the recommended service schedule.
