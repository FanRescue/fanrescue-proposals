Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of what we'll be supplying, installing, and commissioning at 46 Cranbrook Road. The system is grouped into four areas: air handling and ventilation, ductwork and distribution, ancillary systems, and commissioning and handover.

Air Handling & Ventilation

  • Supply & extract motorised ventilation system for the dining area A balanced supply-and-extract system providing general ventilation to the dining area. Motorised supply unit drawing tempered fresh air in, and a separately motorised extract unit removing stale air. Both units sized and selected to suit the room volume and occupancy, with controls allowing the system to be run together or independently.
  • Four-unit ducted air conditioning system Four ducted indoor AC units serving the dining area, providing zoned heating and cooling. F-Gas certified installation including refrigerant pipework, electrical interconnections, condensate drainage, and commissioning under our F-Gas registration (FGAS2001890).

Ductwork & Distribution

  • Ventilation ductwork, diffusers, bends & fitting kit Galvanised ductwork to BESA DW 144 standards serving both supply and extract sides of the ventilation system, including all bends, transitions, supports, and the full fitting kit. Ceiling-mounted diffusers and grilles selected and positioned to give even air distribution across the dining area. Discharge termination at roof / external wall location.
  • AC pipework, diffusers & fitting kit Insulated refrigerant pipework runs between the indoor units and the outdoor condenser, supply and return air diffusers to each indoor unit, and the full bracketry, supports, and fitting kit required for a clean install.

Controls, Electrical & Ancillaries

  • Local electrical connections and controls Local electrical connections from existing isolators / fused spurs (provided by client's electrician) to the ventilation motors and AC indoor / outdoor units. User controls for the AC system positioned for staff access. Discharge end ventilation termination finished and weather-sealed.

Commissioning & Handover

  • Full system commissioning and airflow balancing Once installation is complete, we run all systems up, verify supply and extract performance against design, and balance airflows so every area is properly ventilated and pressure-balanced.
  • BESA-compliant installation documentation Installation carried out under our BESA certification (HV020676) to DW 144 / DW 172 standards. Commissioning certificates issued on handover.
  • Product data sheets and client walkthrough on handover Technical data sheets for all installed equipment provided on the day. We walk you through the systems — how to operate them, how to change filters, what to check day to day, and the recommended service schedule.
Exclusions

Excluded from this quote: any mains electrical work back to the consumer unit (a qualified electrician on your side to provide local isolators and dedicated circuits); structural / builder's works including making good of fabric, ceilings, or paintwork after install; scaffolding or access equipment beyond standard ladders and steps; planning, Building Control submissions, or landlord consents; any out-of-hours or weekend working unless agreed in advance.
