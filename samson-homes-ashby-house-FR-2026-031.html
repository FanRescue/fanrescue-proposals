Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of what we'll be supplying, installing, and commissioning at Ashby House. The system is grouped into four areas: extraction and canopies, fresh-air supply and filtration, ductwork and distribution, and commissioning and handover.

Extraction & Canopies

  • 4 × stainless-steel extraction canopies Four wall-mounted canopies fabricated in stainless steel, positioned over the cooking line. Each canopy is sized to suit the equipment beneath and designed to capture grease, smoke, steam, and combustion gases at source. Supplied with grease filters and integrated lighting. Final sizes and configurations confirmed on site survey.
  • Stainless-steel wall cladding beneath canopies Stainless-steel cladding fitted to the wall area beneath each canopy, providing a hygienic, heat-resistant, and easy-to-clean surface behind the cooking line. Meets food-safe and fire-rated wall surface requirements for commercial kitchens. Cladding extents and joint detailing confirmed on site survey.
  • 630mm extract fan A 630mm centrifugal extract fan handling the kitchen canopy duty, sized to deliver the design extract rate across all four canopies. Speed-controlled to balance against canopy face velocity and paired with appropriate attenuation to keep in-room and external sound levels within acceptable limits.
  • 2 × ESP 7500 electrostatic precipitators Two ESP 7500 electrostatic precipitator units installed in series with the extract system. ESPs remove fine grease particulate and smoke from the extract air before it passes through the carbon stage, dramatically reducing fire risk in downstream ductwork and improving filter life. Industry-leading particulate capture for dense urban sites.

Fresh Air Supply & Filtration

  • 560mm fresh-air supply fan A 560mm fresh-air supply fan delivering tempered make-up air into the kitchen, sized to balance against the extract rate so the kitchen stays neutral or slightly negative — preventing cooking odours migrating into the rest of the building. Speed-controlled and paired with attenuation.
  • Carbon filter bank for odour control Activated carbon filtration installed downstream of the ESP stage to neutralise residual cooking odours before discharge. Critical in mixed-use developments where neighbouring residential or retail tenants share envelope space. Filters are serviceable and replaceable on a planned interval.

Ductwork & Distribution

  • Standard galvanised steel ductwork — not fire-rated All extract, supply, and fresh-air duct runs in galvanised mild steel, fabricated and installed to BESA DW 144 standard. Standard rectangular and circular sections — 2-hour fire-rated ductwork is excluded from this price. If the building's fire strategy requires fire-rated ductwork on any part of the route, that will be priced separately following the site visit.
  • Diffusers, grilles, and discharge termination Supply diffusers and extract grilles selected to suit the kitchen space. Extract discharge routed to the agreed termination point with weatherproof cowl and anti-backdraft protection.
  • Full fitting kit — supports, fixings, flexible connectors Everything needed to install the system properly: duct supports and brackets, anti-vibration mounts on fans and ESP units, flexible connections to prevent noise and vibration transmission, and all associated fixings.

Commissioning & Handover

  • Full system commissioning and airflow balancing Once installation is complete, we run all systems up, verify supply and extract performance against design, and balance airflows so the kitchen is properly ventilated and pressure-balanced. ESP units and carbon filtration verified through their full operating range.
  • BESA-compliant installation documentation Installation carried out under our BESA certification (HV020676) to DW 144 / DW 172 standards. Commissioning certificates issued for the ventilation system on handover.
  • Product data sheets and client walkthrough on handover Technical data sheets for all installed equipment provided on the day. We walk you through the system — how to operate it, how to change filters, what to check day to day, and the recommended service schedule.
Explicit Exclusions & To Be Confirmed

Excluded: 2-hour fire-rated ductwork. If the building's fire strategy requires fire-rated duct on any section of the route, that will be quoted separately after the site visit.

To be confirmed: Ansul (or equivalent) wet-chemical fire suppression system. We can include this if needed once the cooking equipment and insurance requirements are confirmed — priced separately.

Other Exclusions

Building works and structural penetrations beyond those described. Planning permission. Builder's work. Electrical connection to incoming supply, distribution boards, and mains isolators (by others). Gas supply and gas works. Ceiling tile replacements where damaged outside our scope. BMS or building-wide controls integration. Any work not listed above.
