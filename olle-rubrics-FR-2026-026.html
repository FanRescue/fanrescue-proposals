Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of everything being supplied, installed, modified and commissioned at Olle, Battersea Power Station — plus the cleaning of the existing extraction system. Anything not listed below is either explicitly excluded or to be confirmed under the pricing assumption.

Extraction System

  • Supply and installation of new kitchen extraction canopy Stainless steel canopy fabricated and installed over the cooking line, sized for the kitchen layout established at survey. Includes all integral grease filters, lighting, and internal pre-wiring ready for connection.
  • Tie-in to landlord's existing extract fan The new canopy will be connected to the existing landlord-owned extract fan. Tie-in includes appropriate adaptor work, sealed transitions, and verification that the existing fan can deliver the design extract rate for the new canopy.
  • Tie-in to landlord's existing ESP filtration unit The new system routes through the existing ESP filtration. We confirm correct sealing, airflow direction, and access for ongoing maintenance. Operation of the ESP unit itself remains under the landlord's maintenance regime.

Ductwork & Distribution

  • Ductwork modifications Modification and adaptation of the existing ductwork to suit the new canopy connection — re-routing where required, additional supports, sealed joints, and adjustments to maintain airflow integrity through to the existing fan and ESP.
  • Spigot panel fabrication and installation Fabrication and installation of spigot panels at the canopy and ductwork connection points. Sized to match canopy take-off and existing duct geometry, with sealed flanges and secure mechanical fixings.
  • Access doors at TR19-compliant intervals Where ductwork is being modified, access doors are fitted at the spacing required by BESA TR19 to allow proper future cleaning and inspection. This protects you for compliance and insurance purposes long-term.

Testing & Commissioning

  • Full system commissioning and airflow testing Once installation is complete, we run the system up, test extraction performance against the design figures, and verify that the existing fan and ESP are delivering correctly with the new canopy load. The system is left working — not just installed.
  • Sealed-joint and pressure verification All modified ductwork joints are checked for leakage. Any losses are identified and resolved before sign-off so the system performs to spec from day one.

Existing Extraction System Clean (Pre-Handover)

  • TR19-compliant deep clean of existing ductwork, canopy and accessible plant A full BESA TR19-compliant deep clean of the existing extraction system before the new canopy is brought online. Removal of accumulated grease and contamination from all accessible duct runs, canopy plenum, filters and connections.
  • Photographic before/after evidence and TR19 certification Photo documentation of accessible duct interiors before and after cleaning. TR19 certificate issued on completion — required for EHO compliance and to keep your insurance valid.
  • Coordination with installation programme The clean is sequenced with the install so the system is presented in clean, certified condition at the point the new canopy is commissioned and handed over.
Exclusions

Replacement, upgrade, recommissioning or remedial works to the landlord's existing extract fan or existing ESP filtration unit. Any structural alterations, builder's work, or planning consents. Gas supply works. Fire suppression systems. Electrical connection from incoming building supply (by others). Any non-accessible duct sections or concealed plant identified during cleaning that require additional access works. Anything not explicitly listed in the scope above.
