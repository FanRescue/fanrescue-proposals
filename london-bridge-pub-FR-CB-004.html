What's Included

Scope of the Clean

Every clean — whether one-off or under a service agreement — covers the full extraction system to TR19 standard at Bridget Street.

TR19 Deep Clean

  • Canopy and plenum deep clean Full degreasing of the canopy interior and exterior, plenum chamber, and all accessible surfaces. Grease deposits removed back to clean metal.
  • Ductwork cleaned to TR19 standard Accessible ductwork cleaned along its full run. Where access panels are limited, we install additional access doors where necessary (advised before works) to ensure full compliance.
  • Extract fan cleaned and checked The extract fan impeller and housing are degreased and the fan checked for safe operation. Any developing faults flagged to you in writing.

Certification & Records

  • TR19 cleaning certificate issued A full TR19 Grease cleaning certificate issued on completion — the document your insurer and Environmental Health require to evidence compliance.
  • Before-and-after photographic record Photographs of every section cleaned, with access points documented. A complete visual record kept on file and provided to you.
