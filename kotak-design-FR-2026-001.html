What's Included

Proposed System & Scope of Works

Based on the survey, here's the full scope of what we're quoting. This covers everything needed to deliver a compliant, fully operational ventilation system for Kotak Design.

Supply & Installation

  • Supply and installation of approximately 5600mm x 1200mm canopy hood, connected to existing 2x spigot points
  • Supply and installation of approximately 4500mm x 1200mm canopy hood with full extraction ductwork through to flat roof
  • New ductwork from Canopy 2 to be connected to existing extraction ductwork at roof level
  • Supply and installation of condenser canopy for the dishwasher area
  • Remove and dispose of existing extraction fan
  • Supply and install high-pressure German box fan with 3-phase inverter
  • 2x reducers
  • 1x new ductwork section connecting to the new fan
  • Rubbers and flexible connections
  • Silencer fitted after the fan to reduce noise at discharge
  • Upgrade fresh air (make-up air) fan to suit the new kitchen requirements

Commissioning & Handover

  • System commissioning and airflow balancing
  • Fan speed and inverter control settings confirmed
  • Efflux velocity check and documentation
  • Noise assessment at boundary (where required)
  • As-fitted drawings supplied on completion
  • O&M manual and product data sheets
  • Client handover and system walkthrough
Exclusions

This quotation excludes: electrical connection to the fan (by others unless otherwise agreed), building works and structural penetrations beyond those described above, planning permission (if required — Fan Rescue can advise), gas work, and fire suppression systems.
