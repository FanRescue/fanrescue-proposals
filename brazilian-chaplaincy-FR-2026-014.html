Site Assessment

What We Found

Based on our assessment of the photos from the extraction system at Underwood Road, London E1 5AW, here’s our view of your current position.

!
Heavy Grease Buildup on Baffle Filters

The stainless steel baffle filters are showing significant grease deposits across the full width of the canopy. This restricts airflow through the filter bank, reduces extraction efficiency, and creates a fire hazard. All filters need removing, deep-cleaning, and refitting as part of the service.

!
Canopy Internals Require Deep Clean

Grease has accumulated on the canopy plenum ceiling and interior surfaces behind the baffle filters. The built-in canopy light is also partially obscured by grease deposits. A surface wipe is not sufficient — the canopy internals need a full strip-down and degrease to TR19 standard.

!
Rooftop Extract Fan & Ductwork

The rooftop extract fan unit and galvanised ductwork are accessible via the flat roof. The fan unit, ductwork internals, and discharge point all need cleaning and inspection to ensure full airflow and TR19 compliance from canopy to discharge.

✓
System Components in Serviceable Condition

The canopy, baffle filters, ductwork, and rooftop fan unit are all structurally sound and in serviceable condition. No replacement parts are anticipated — the system simply needs a thorough clean.

What This Means

Your system needs a full TR19-compliant deep clean — canopy internals, ductwork, and fan unit. A proper clean will restore extraction performance, reduce fire risk, and give you the documentation your insurer and EHO need to see.

What’s Included

Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of the cleaning service proposed for your extraction system at Underwood Road, London E1 5AW. All work is carried out in accordance with TR19 guidelines and current fire safety regulations.

Extraction System Cleaning

  • Full internal clean of all extraction ductwork from canopy to discharge point — complete degreasing of the full duct run using industry-approved methods and chemicals, all accessible sections cleaned to TR19 standard
  • Canopy deep clean and degreasing — full strip-down and degreasing of canopy internals including baffles, plenums, grease channels, and built-in lighting, external surfaces cleaned and polished
  • Rooftop fan unit strip-down, clean, and reassembly — fan impeller, casing, and motor housing fully degreased, checked for wear, reassembled, and tested on completion
  • Baffle filter removal, deep clean, and refit — all stainless steel baffle filters removed, degreased, and refitted
  • Access panel inspection and cleaning of all accessible sections — all existing access panels opened, inspected, and cleaned, any inaccessible areas noted in the report
  • External discharge point and terminal cleaned and inspected

Documentation & Certification

  • Full photographic report with before and after images — detailed photographic evidence of system condition before and after cleaning, provided digitally within 48 hours of payment
  • TR19 compliance certificate — issued upon receipt of payment, confirms your extraction system has been cleaned to TR19 standard as required by your EHO and insurer
  • Grease depth readings logged and recorded
  • Any access issues or remedial works flagged in writing
  • Digital copies stored for your records — certificates suitable for insurers and EHO inspections
