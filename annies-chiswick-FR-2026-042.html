Why It Matters

TR19 Compliance — The Three Reasons

Kitchen extraction cleaning isn't just housekeeping. It's a fire safety, insurance, and EHO obligation rolled into one.

Fire Safety

Grease accumulation in ductwork is the leading cause of commercial kitchen extraction fires. TR19 cleaning removes the fuel source and dramatically reduces the risk of a catastrophic incident.

Insurance Validity

Most commercial insurance policies require evidence of TR19-compliant cleaning at the recommended frequency. Without a current certificate, a fire claim can be denied outright.

EHO & Landlord Requirements

Environmental Health Officers and most landlords now ask to see a current TR19 certificate during inspections. Non-compliance can affect your hygiene rating and lease standing.

Our Approach

A full TR19-standard clean covers the canopy internals, the full duct run from canopy to discharge point, and a complete fan unit strip-down. Nothing surface-level — the photographic report and compliance certificate prove it.

What's Included

Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of the cleaning service proposed for your extraction system at 162 Thames Road. All work is carried out in accordance with TR19 guidelines and current fire safety regulations.

Extraction System Cleaning

  • Full internal clean of all extraction ductwork from canopy to discharge point — ground-floor horizontal duct, kitchen-to-first-floor riser, first-floor cross-run, and final riser to roof termination, all degreased to TR19 standard
  • Canopy deep clean and degreasing — full strip-down and degreasing of canopy internals, baffles, plenums, and grease channels, external S/S surfaces cleaned and polished
  • S/S baffle filters removed, cleaned, refitted — existing filters cleaned on the day; replacement filters supplied and fitted (included in the pricing below)
  • Sound attenuator and flexi couplings inspected and cleaned — first-floor attenuator wiped through, flexi joint condition checked and photographed
  • IPH inline fan unit strip-down, clean, and reassembly — fan impeller, casing, motor housing and filter box fully degreased, checked for wear, reassembled, and tested on completion
  • All existing access doors used and cleaned around — 400×400 door at base of riser in kitchen and 450×450 door in staff changing area
  • External roof discharge point and terminal cleaned and inspected — safe ladder access managed by our team, no scaffolding required

Documentation & Reporting

  • Full photographic report with before-and-after images — detailed photographic evidence of system condition before and after cleaning, provided digitally within 48 hours of payment
  • TR19 compliance certificate — issued upon receipt of payment, confirms your extraction system has been cleaned to TR19 standard as required by your EHO and insurer
  • Grease depth readings logged and recorded
  • Anything found on the day reported back in writing — perished flexi couplings, worn seals, damaged ducting or access concerns photographed and flagged with recommended remedial action
  • Digital copies stored for your records — certificates suitable for insurers and EHO inspections
