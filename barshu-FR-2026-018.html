Site Assessment

What We Found

Based on our review of the video walkthrough, system diagram, and previous TR19 certificate you provided for the extraction system at 28 Frith Street, here’s our assessment.

!
High-Duty Wok Cooking — Quarterly Cleaning Required

Chinese wok cooking at this volume produces significant airborne grease and particulates. Under TR19 guidance, heavy-duty operations (woks, chargrills, high-output gas burners) fall into the highest cleaning frequency bracket. A quarterly cycle is the right call to stay compliant and keep fire risk under control.

✓
Main Canopy & Internal Ductwork — Fully Accessible

The 8-filter canopy, baffle filters, and main horizontal/vertical duct runs are all accessible from inside the kitchen. The diagram shows adequate access panels along the duct route, and the video confirms the main ductwork can be cleaned to a full TR19 standard.

✓
Stainless Steel Baffle Filters — In Place & Serviceable

All 8 baffle grease filters on the canopy are fitted and in good condition. These will be removed, soaked, degreased, and refitted on each visit.

!
External Fan Unit & Roof Discharge — Excluded From This Quote

You’ve flagged that the platform giving access to the external fan unit is unstable, and the previous TR19 certificate also omitted this section. We’ve taken that area out of the pricing for now while our team finalises the safest access method. We’ll come back to you separately on this.

!
Previous TR19 Certificate — Coverage Gap Noted

The previous certificate covered the canopy and internal ductwork but did not include the external fan and roof-side discharge. For a fully compliant TR19 certificate, the whole extraction run must be cleaned and documented. We’ll resolve this on a second phase once the access question is answered.

What This Means

The accessible portion of your system — canopy, baffles, internal ductwork, and inline fan — can be brought up to full TR19 standard on a quarterly cycle under this proposal. The external fan and roof discharge will be handled separately once we’ve confirmed safe access. The end goal is one clean system, one clean certificate.

What’s Included

Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of the cleaning service proposed for the accessible sections of your extraction system at 28 Frith Street. All work is carried out in accordance with TR19 guidelines and current fire safety regulations.

Extraction System Cleaning

  • Canopy deep clean & degreasing — full strip-down of canopy internals including plenums, grease channels, and interior surfaces, external stainless surfaces cleaned and polished
  • Baffle filter removal, deep clean & refit — all 8 stainless steel baffle filters removed, soaked, degreased, and refitted
  • Horizontal ductwork clean — full internal degreasing of the ~5m horizontal duct run from canopy to vertical riser, all accessible sections cleaned to TR19 standard
  • Vertical riser clean — full internal degreasing of the ~3.5m vertical riser up to the inline fan unit
  • Inline fan unit clean — fan impeller, casing, and motor housing fully degreased via existing access hatch, checked for wear and rebalance, reinstated and tested on completion
  • Access panel inspection — all existing access panels opened, inspected, cleaned, and resealed; any additional access requirements noted in writing

Documentation & Certification

  • Full photographic report — detailed before-and-after imagery of every cleaned section, provided digitally within 48 hours of payment
  • TR19 compliance certificate — issued upon receipt of payment, confirms the scoped sections have been cleaned to TR19 standard for your EHO and insurer
  • Grease depth readings — logged and recorded against each access point
  • Access-limited areas flagged in writing — including the external fan and roof discharge, clearly noted on the certificate while that section remains out of scope
  • Digital copies stored for your records — certificates suitable for insurance renewals and EHO inspections
