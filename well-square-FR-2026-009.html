Scope of Works

What's Included

This quote covers two interconnected packages of work. Package A addresses fresh air ventilation via new AHU systems; Package B addresses the heating and cooling distribution to existing FCUs. Both are scoped, priced, and delivered together.

Package A — AHU Heat Exchange Ventilation Systems £16,600 + VAT

AHU Equipment Supply & Installation
  • Supply and installation of 2 no. Daikin heat exchange AHU ventilation units Two Daikin packaged heat recovery AHUs sized to serve the co-working area. Heat exchange units recover warmth from extracted stale air and transfer it to incoming fresh air — reducing heating load and improving thermal comfort year-round.
  • Unit positioning, structural support, and fitting kit installation Each unit installed with full manufacturer fitting kit including anti-vibration mounts, supports, and fixings. Positioned to allow adequate service access and meet manufacturer clearance requirements.
  • Controls and controllers — supply, wiring, and configuration Daikin controllers supplied and installed for each unit. Wired and configured to allow user-adjustable speed and mode settings. Controllers positioned for convenient access within the occupied space.
Extract Ductwork System
  • Extract ductwork system — full distribution network Complete extract ductwork from the occupied areas back to the AHU extract connections. Sized and routed to achieve required airflow rates and serve all extract points within the co-working space.
  • Extract valve grilles — supply and fix at all extract points Adjustable extract valve grilles installed at ceiling level throughout the space. Valves allow individual airflow adjustment and are set during commissioning to achieve balanced extraction across all zones.
  • Volume control dampers (VCDs) throughout extract network VCDs installed at key distribution points within the extract ductwork to allow accurate balancing of airflows across the system. Essential for achieving the designed extract rates at each point.
Supply Ductwork System
  • Supply ductwork system — full distribution from AHU to all supply points Complete supply air ductwork from the AHU discharge connections to all supply points throughout the co-working area. Insulated where required to prevent condensation and maintain supply air temperature.
  • Air diffuser grilles — supply and fix at all supply points Ceiling-mounted air diffuser grilles installed at all supply points. Diffusers selected to achieve even air distribution at low velocity — maintaining occupant comfort without draughts or noise at design airflow rates.
  • All ductwork brackets, supports, and fixings throughout All support brackets, hangers, and fixings required to securely support both extract and supply ductwork. Installed at correct centres to meet BESA DW144 guidance and prevent deflection or vibration in service.
Testing & Commissioning — AHU Systems
  • Full system test and commissioning of both AHU ventilation systems Both AHU units run up and commissioned on completion. Extract and supply airflows measured and balanced at every grille and valve. Systems set to design duty and verified before handover. Commissioning data recorded and issued.

Package B — LTHW & CHW System Recommissioning to FCUs £12,150 + VAT

FCU Pipework & Isolation
  • Drain system and disconnect all non-HVAC items from FCUs All existing FCU connections drained and non-HVAC items removed from the circuit prior to recommissioning. This ensures clean, correctly configured connections at every FCU before new pipework and valves are installed.
  • Supply and installation of adequate HVAC isolation valves to all FCUs Properly rated HVAC-grade isolation valves installed at each FCU on both flow and return pipework. Correct isolation valves are essential for future servicing — allowing individual FCUs to be isolated without shutting down the whole circuit.
  • New pipework connections to all FCUs — no flexible hoses All FCU connections made with hard copper or steel pipework as appropriate. Flexible pipe connections are not used — rigid pipework with proper support gives a durable, leak-free installation and meets good practice standards.
  • Commission valves installed and set throughout Commissioning (balancing) valves installed and set on the circuit to achieve correct differential pressures across all FCUs. Properly balanced circuits ensure each FCU receives its design flow rate — preventing hot/cold complaints and uneven conditioning.
  • Vent points installed where required Air vents installed at high points in the pipework circuit where air locks could form. Manual or automatic vents installed as appropriate to ensure circuits remain fully charged and free of airlock.
Actuators, Controls & Wiring
  • Check all actuators and controls on existing FCUs All existing FCU actuators inspected and tested. Non-functioning or incorrectly operating actuators identified and reported. Replacement actuators supplied and fitted where required to reinstate correct operation.
  • Control wiring installation to communicate with temperature controllers Control wiring installed between temperature controllers and FCU actuators/controls throughout the space. Correct wiring configuration ensures the temperature control system can send heating and cooling demands to each FCU automatically.
  • Automation of heat and cool demand signals across all FCUs Controls configured so that the temperature controllers automatically switch between heating (LTHW) and cooling (CHW) modes in response to space temperature. Removes the need for manual switching and ensures efficient, responsive conditioning throughout the co-working area.
System Commissioning
  • Full commissioning of LTHW and CHW circuits to all FCUs Both heating and cooling circuits commissioned on completion. Flow rates verified, controls tested through full heating and cooling cycles. Systems left fully operational with all FCUs responding correctly to demand signals before handover.

Handover — Both Packages

  • As-fitted drawings issued on completion Record drawings covering AHU ductwork layout, FCU pipework configuration, valve positions, and control wiring routes. Issued on the day of handover and required for future maintenance and compliance records.
  • Product data sheets for all installed equipment Technical data sheets for both Daikin AHU units, controllers, valves, and associated components provided on completion. Includes manufacturer maintenance schedules and warranty information.
  • Client walkthrough and system demonstration Our engineer walks you through both systems before leaving — how to operate the AHU controllers, what the controls do, how to identify any faults, and what routine maintenance checks are required.
