What’s Included

Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of services proposed for the kitchen at 28 Beaufort Street. Cleaning is carried out in accordance with TR19 guidelines; the gas safety inspection is carried out under the CP42 standard by our Gas Safe registered engineer.

Kitchen Extraction Cleaning (TR19)

  • Full internal clean of all extraction ductwork from canopy to discharge point — complete degreasing of the duct run using industry-approved methods and chemicals, all accessible sections cleaned to TR19 standard
  • Canopy deep clean and degreasing — full strip-down of canopy internals including baffles, plenums, and grease channels, external surfaces cleaned and polished
  • Fan unit strip-down, clean, and reassembly — impeller, casing, and motor housing fully degreased, checked for wear, reassembled, and tested on completion
  • Baffle filter removal, deep clean, and refit — all stainless steel baffle filters removed, degreased, and refitted
  • Access panel inspection and cleaning of all accessible sections — existing panels opened and inspected, any inaccessible areas noted in the report
  • External discharge point and terminal cleaned and inspected

Gas Appliance Safety Inspection (CP42)

  • Full visual and operational inspection of all commercial gas appliances in the kitchen — ranges, ovens, fryers, hobs and any other gas-fed equipment
  • Tightness test of the kitchen gas installation — pipework checked for leaks under operating pressure
  • Combustion analysis on each appliance — flue gas readings taken to confirm safe combustion and correct burner operation
  • Ventilation and interlock checks — confirms extraction is operating correctly when gas appliances are in use, in line with BS 6173
  • Isolation valve and emergency shut-off check
  • CP42 commercial gas safety record issued — signed by our Gas Safe registered engineer, suitable for your insurer and EHO records

Documentation & Certification

  • Full photographic report with before and after images — provided digitally within 48 hours of payment
  • TR19 compliance certificate issued on receipt of payment
  • CP42 commercial gas safety record issued on the day
  • Grease depth readings logged and recorded
  • Any access issues or remedial works flagged in writing
  • Digital copies stored for your records — suitable for insurers and EHO inspections

Site-Specific Notes for Allen Hall

  • Both services carried out on the same visit where practical — minimises kitchen downtime and one site attendance covers both
  • Work scheduled outside of seminary catering hours by agreement — evenings or early mornings available
  • Single point of contact for scheduling: Sam Matthews

  • If access is restricted to any duct section, this will be noted on the report so you have a written record for your insurer
