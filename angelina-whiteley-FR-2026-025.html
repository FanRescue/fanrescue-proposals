Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full mechanical package being supplied, installed and commissioned at Unit R01. Pricing reflects the indicative scope to date — final figures are subject to design approval.

LTHW / CHW 4-Pipe Ducted Air Conditioning System

£102,030 ex VAT
  • Supply & installation of 7 No. 4-pipe LTHW/CHW ducted air conditioning units Seven 4-pipe LTHW/CHW ducted-type fan coil units serving the ground floor and basement areas, running directly off the landlord's system as per the landlord's technical agents' confirmation. Each unit zoned, sized to load, and installed with the appropriate air-side and water-side ancillaries.
  • Full ductwork distribution & air diffuser grilles All associated supply and return ductwork from each fan coil unit out to ceiling-mounted diffuser grilles selected to suit the layout and air pattern. Ductwork sized for the design airflow and installed in accordance with BESA DW 144.
  • HVAC pipework — copper pressfit (no hot works) All LTHW and CHW pipework run in copper pressfit fittings — avoids hot works on site, faster install, fully sealed and pressure-tested. Sized to the design flow rates with appropriate pipe routing back to the landlord's risers.
  • Isolation valves, AAVs, strainers & pipework ancillaries Isolation valves at each unit and at branch tees for service access without draining the main system. Automatic air vents (AAVs) at high points, strainers on the supply side to protect the unit coils, and all the unions, drains and commissioning fittings required for proper operation.
  • Pipework insulation & protection All HVAC pipework lagged with closed-cell elastomeric insulation throughout — sized appropriately for hot and chilled service to prevent heat loss/gain and condensation. Vapour barrier maintained at all joints and penetrations.
  • Temperature controllers & zone control Temperature controller at each unit/zone for accurate set-point control of heating and cooling. Wall-mounted thermostats positioned for representative sensing in each zone.
  • Cleaning, flushing & commissioning of the water system Full chemical cleaning and flushing of the LTHW and CHW pipework before commissioning — removes installation debris and conditions the system for long-term reliability. Flow rates set, AAVs vented, balance verified and commissioning records issued in line with BSRIA guidance.

General Ventilation Heat Exchange Ventilation Air Handling Units (AHU) System

£27,840 ex VAT
  • Supply & installation of 4 No. Daikin air handling units Daikin AHUs delivered, set in position and anti-vibration mounted on isolated frames. Connected to the supply/extract ductwork via flexible canvas connectors to break vibration and noise transmission into the duct system.
  • Filtration, heating/cooling coils & controls integration Each AHU comes with the appropriate filtration class, integrated heating and cooling coils as specified, and full controls integration with the HVAC panel — including airflow monitoring, filter pressure switches and frost protection where applicable.
  • Fire dampers fitted at all duct penetrations through fire compartmentation Fire-rated dampers (typically 60 or 120 minute rating to suit the compartmentation strategy) installed where ductwork crosses fire-rated walls or floors. Drop-test certified at install, accessible for annual statutory testing, fitted in accordance with BS 9999 and manufacturer instructions. Fire-stopping at the penetration sleeve included.
  • Anti-vibration mounts, isolators & full installation kit All AVMs, electrical isolators, condensate connections, drainage trays and the support steelwork required to install each AHU correctly and quietly. Complete package.

Kitchen Extraction System

£37,210 ex VAT
  • Stainless steel extraction canopy(s) over cooking line Grade 304 stainless steel canopy(s) sized and positioned to fully cover the cooking equipment with correct overhang. Internal grease baffle filters, integral lighting and condensate gutters as standard. Supplied with all hanging brackets, drop rods and fixing kits.
  • High-pressure German extraction fan, sized and selected to suit the job High-pressure centrifugal extraction fan — premium German manufacture (e.g. Nicotra, Helios or equivalent) — selected to match the calculated system resistance and design extraction volume with headroom. Inverter-driven for fine commissioning and energy efficiency. Comes complete with motor, drive, weather cowl and IP-rated terminal box.
  • Odour control system to neutralise cooking emissions Inline odour control unit fitted upstream of discharge — typically carbon filtration with optional UV/ozone stage depending on cuisine and discharge location. Sized to handle full extraction volume, with access doors for filter replacement and a service log to maintain compliance with planning conditions.
  • Acoustic attenuators sized to the fan Inline rectangular attenuators (suction and/or discharge side as required by the acoustic design) sized to the fan and the noise-sensitive boundaries. Matched specifically to the fan's frequency profile to drop breakout noise to the design dB(A) target — protects neighbours, planning compliance and the kitchen environment.
  • Fully welded grease-rated ductwork to discharge Fabricated and installed in accordance with BESA DW 172 — fully welded seams, correct material thickness, access panels at code-mandated intervals for TR19 cleaning, and routed efficiently to the roof discharge point.
  • Anti-vibration mounts, flexible connectors, isolators & fixing kits All AVMs (spring or rubber-in-shear as appropriate), flexible canvas connectors at the fan inlet/outlet, electrical isolator at the fan, and the full kit of brackets, drop rods, threaded studs and fasteners required to support the system properly. Nothing left for "others to provide".
  • Make-up air integration with extraction Balanced supply air to match extraction volume — prevents negative pressure in the kitchen and keeps the extraction system working as designed. Supply terminations positioned to avoid disrupting capture velocity at the canopy.

Kitchen Fresh Air System

£11,250 ex VAT
  • High-pressure supply air fan, sized and selected to suit the job High-pressure centrifugal supply fan delivering tempered fresh air to the kitchen to balance the extraction system, selected against the calculated system resistance with headroom for filter loading. Inverter-driven, weatherproofed where externally mounted.
  • Acoustic attenuators sized to the fan Inline attenuators on the supply side, matched to the fan's frequency profile to drop both breakout noise and discharge noise into the demise to the design dB(A) target — keeps the front-of-house environment quiet.
  • Anti-vibration mounts, flexible connectors, isolators & fixing kits Full set of AVMs, flexible canvas connectors at the fan, electrical isolator and all support steelwork, brackets, drop rods and fasteners required to install the system properly. Complete package — nothing left out.

Toilet Extract System

£3,490 ex VAT
  • High-pressure extract fan, sized and selected to suit the job Inline centrifugal extract fan(s) serving the WC areas, sized for the design extraction rate to comply with Building Regs Part F. Quiet-running and accessible for service.
  • Acoustic attenuators sized to the fan Inline attenuators matched to the fan to control noise breakout into the WC areas and adjacent spaces — particularly important given proximity to dining/lounge areas.
  • Ductwork, grilles & weatherproof discharge Galvanised ductwork, ceiling extract grilles and routed to a roof discharge with weather cowl, complying with Part F separation distances from openable windows and intakes.
  • Anti-vibration mounts, flexible connectors, isolators & fixing kits All AVMs, flexible connectors at the fan, electrical isolator and the full kit of supports, brackets, drop rods and fasteners. Complete and ready to commission.

Door Heater / Air Curtain

£5,480 ex VAT
  • Heated air curtain at main entrance Recessed or surface-mounted heated air curtain sized to the door opening, supplied and fitted above the entrance. Maintains internal comfort, reduces air infiltration and protects the conditioned space from draughts and external pollution. Includes mounting brackets, electrical connection point and remote controller.

Ongoing Maintenance

Your System Will Need Regular TR19 Cleaning

Once installed, the kitchen extraction system must be cleaned at TR19-compliant intervals to remain EHO-compliant and keep your insurance valid. Grease build-up in uncleaned ductwork is a fire risk and a compliance failure — Angelina's expected covers will likely place this at a quarterly frequency.

Fan Rescue runs TR19 cleaning contracts across London. We'll already know your system inside-out — let us maintain it. We'll recommend the right cleaning frequency once we've seen how the kitchen runs in operation.
