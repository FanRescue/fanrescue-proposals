What We Found

Based on the video walkthroughs of both kitchens at 65–75 King Street, here’s our view of the current extraction systems and what a full TR19-compliant service would cover.

✓
Stainless Steel Canopies — Good Physical Condition

Both sites have well-specified stainless steel canopies with accessible filter banks. Externally they’re clean and well-maintained — a credit to whoever has been looking after them. Physical condition of the stainless is excellent on both kitchens.

✓
Mixed Filter Types — Both Serviceable

One site uses perforated plate supply plenum filters with a secondary canopy section; the other uses traditional stainless steel baffle filters. All are removable, in serviceable condition, and will be stripped, degreased, and refitted as part of every clean.

!
Light-to-Moderate Grease on Filter Surfaces

On closer view of the baffle filters and plenum undersides, there is light-to-moderate grease accumulation — consistent with a kitchen that’s being maintained, but indicating a clean is due or soon-due. A full TR19 deep-clean will reset both systems and give you a documented baseline.

!
Internal Ductwork & Fan Units — Not Visible in Walk-Through

The ductwork runs and fan units aren’t visible in the videos, which is normal — they’re the part that gets missed on a surface-level clean. Internal duct grease and fan impeller build-up are the main fire risk and the main reason TR19 certification matters. Our scope always includes full access-panel strip, internal duct degrease, and fan unit strip-clean-reassemble.

✓
Two Distinct Kitchen Systems — Priced Individually

The Gurudwara kitchen and the Afghan Ekta kitchen are separate systems with their own canopies, ductwork and fans. Each is quoted and cleaned as a standalone job, with its own TR19 certificate and photographic report per visit.

What This Means

Both kitchens are in solid physical condition. Whoever is currently cleaning for you is doing a reasonable job on the surfaces — but surface cleaning isn’t TR19 compliance. What’s Included

Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of the cleaning service proposed for each of your kitchens at 65–75 King Street, Southall — covering both the Gurudwara Guru Nanak Darbar kitchen and the Afghan Ekta kitchen. The same scope applies to both sites, priced per site per visit. All work is carried out in accordance with TR19 guidelines and current fire safety regulations.

Extraction System Cleaning

  • Full internal clean of all extraction ductwork from canopy to discharge point — complete degreasing of the full duct run using industry-approved methods and chemicals, all accessible sections cleaned to TR19 standard
  • Canopy deep clean and degreasing — full strip-down and degreasing of canopy internals including baffles, plenums, and grease channels, external surfaces cleaned and polished
  • Fan unit strip-down, clean, and reassembly — fan impeller, casing, and motor housing fully degreased, checked for wear, reassembled, and tested on completion
  • Filter removal, deep clean, and refit — all baffle and/or perforated plate filters removed, degreased, and refitted
  • Access panel inspection and cleaning of all accessible sections — all existing access panels opened, inspected, and cleaned, any inaccessible areas noted in the report
  • External discharge point and terminal cleaned and inspected

Documentation & Certification (Per Site)

  • Full photographic report with before and after images — detailed photographic evidence of system condition before and after cleaning, issued per site, per visit, provided digitally within 48 hours of payment
  • TR19 compliance certificate per site — one certificate issued per kitchen, per visit, on receipt of payment. Confirms the extraction system has been cleaned to TR19 standard as required by your EHO and insurer
  • Grease depth readings logged and recorded
  • Any access issues or remedial works flagged in writing
  • Digital copies stored for your records — certificates suitable for insurers and EHO inspections
