Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of what we'll be carrying out at 5 Leonard Street. The relocation works are common to both options — the difference is the replacement fan and controller specification. You choose one of the two options; both are described below and priced separately in the Investment section.

Fan Relocation & Common Works

  • Removal of existing extraction fan from current position Safe disconnection and removal of the existing fan unit from its current mounting. Power isolated, electrical connections terminated safely, and the unit removed without disturbing surrounding ductwork.
  • Relocation and remounting at agreed new position The fan is repositioned to the new agreed location. Mounting brackets, anti-vibration mounts and weatherproofing reinstated as required. Position confirmed with you on site before final fix.
  • Reconnection to existing ductwork Flexible connectors and duct adaptors as needed to tie the relocated fan back into the existing extract ductwork without imposing stress on the duct runs. Joints sealed and secured to maintain extract performance.
  • Same-day delivery of replacement equipment All replacement equipment delivered to site on the day of works. £150 + VAT same-day delivery charge applies to both options to keep the schedule tight and avoid a return visit.

Option 1 — Like-for-Like Replacement

  • Supply and install identical replacement extraction fan Direct like-for-like replacement matching the existing unit's specification — same airflow, same physical envelope, same mounting pattern. Minimum disruption, fastest route back to full service.
  • Retain and reuse existing fan controller The existing controller is retained and reconnected to the new fan. No change to the way the system is operated day to day. Continuity for staff who already know how to use it.

Option 2 — Modernised Replacement with Inverter Controller

  • Supply and install modern replacement extraction fan A current-generation extraction fan delivering the same duty as your existing unit but with improved motor efficiency and reliability. Suitable for sustained commercial use, with longer expected service life than the unit being replaced.
  • Replace existing controller with a digital 3-phase inverter The existing controller is removed and replaced with a digital 3-phase inverter drive. This gives variable-speed control of the fan, soft-start to reduce mechanical and electrical stress on startup, and better long-term reliability than a traditional fixed-speed setup. Variable speed also means you can run the fan at lower speeds during quieter periods and ramp up during service — quieter operation and lower energy use.
  • Wire and configure inverter controller Inverter wired in to the existing power supply, configured for the new fan's parameters, and tested through its full speed range before handover.

Commissioning & Handover

  • Full system commissioning and run-test Once installation is complete, we run the fan up, verify extract performance, check vibration levels and run-current, and confirm the system is operating correctly across its full duty range.
  • Walkthrough and handover We walk you through how to operate the new equipment — including the inverter controller if you choose Option 2. Any product data sheets and operating instructions handed over on the day.
Exclusions

Building works and structural penetrations beyond those described. Any modifications to the existing ductwork beyond reconnection at the fan. Upgrade of the incoming electrical supply. Builder's work and making good of finishes. Any work not listed above.
