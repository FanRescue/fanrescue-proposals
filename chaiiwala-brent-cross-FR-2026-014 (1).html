Current Situation & Compliance Position

Based on the TR19 report from your previous provider (Graftons, June 2025) and the information you’ve shared, here’s where things stand.

!
Previous Clean Now Over 9 Months Ago

Your last TR19 clean was in June 2025. For a kitchen operating at light-to-medium grease output on a 6-monthly cycle, you’re now overdue. An expired TR19 certificate leaves you without valid compliance documentation for your insurer or EHO.

!
Access Limitations Flagged

The previous report noted limited access to the fan unit and parts of the ductwork, including corners. Additional access panels were recommended. We’ll assess during the clean and advise on what’s needed — if additional panels are required, this would be quoted separately.

!
Pre-Clean Grease Readings Were High

The June 2025 report recorded pre-clean wet film thickness readings of 600+ microns across the canopy and ductwork. The TR19 threshold for urgent cleaning action is 500 microns. The system was heavily loaded before the last clean, which underlines the importance of maintaining a regular cleaning schedule.

✓
System in Good Overall Condition

The previous report confirmed the ductwork (rectangular and circular) is in good condition, canopy is in good visual condition, fans are working, and baffle filters are serviceable. No leaks or structural issues flagged. This is a well-maintained system that just needs regular professional cleaning to stay compliant.

What’s Included

Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of the cleaning service proposed for your extraction system at Unit 6B, Brent Cross Shopping Centre. All work is carried out in accordance with TR19 guidelines and current fire safety regulations.

Extraction System Cleaning

  • Full internal clean of all extraction ductwork from canopy to discharge point — complete degreasing of the full duct run using industry-approved methods and chemicals, all accessible sections cleaned to TR19 standard
  • Canopy deep clean and degreasing — full strip-down and degreasing of canopy internals including plenums and grease channels, external surfaces cleaned and polished
  • Fan unit strip-down, clean, and reassembly — fan impeller, casing, and motor housing fully degreased, checked for wear, reassembled, and tested on completion
  • Baffle filter removal, deep clean, and refit — all stainless steel baffle filters removed, degreased, and refitted
  • Access panel inspection and cleaning of all accessible sections — all existing access panels and quick-release doors opened, inspected, and cleaned. Any inaccessible areas noted in the report
  • External discharge point and terminal cleaned and inspected

Documentation & Certification

  • Full photographic report with before and after images — detailed photographic evidence of system condition before and after cleaning, provided digitally within 48 hours of payment
  • TR19 compliance certificate — issued upon receipt of payment, confirms your extraction system has been cleaned to TR19 standard as required by your EHO and insurer
  • Wet film thickness (grease depth) readings logged and recorded — pre and post-clean measurements documented
  • Any access issues or remedial works flagged in writing
  • Digital copies stored for your records — certificates suitable for insurers, EHO inspections, and shopping centre management
