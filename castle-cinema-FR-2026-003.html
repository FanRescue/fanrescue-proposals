Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of everything being supplied, installed, and commissioned at The Castle Cinema. This is a complete system — nothing is left out of the price unless stated under Exclusions.

Extraction System

  • Supply and installation of 1300 x 900mm 304-grade stainless steel extraction canopy hood Sized to cover the cooking equipment in position. 304-grade stainless is the commercial kitchen standard — corrosion resistant, easy to clean, and built to last. Includes grease baffles as standard.
  • Supply and installation of high-pressure centrifugal fan with inverter drive Specified to overcome the static pressure resistance of the 16-metre duct run. The inverter gives you variable speed control — turn it down during quieter periods to reduce noise and energy use, up during full service.
  • Supply and installation of in-line acoustic silencer Fitted in the duct run between the fan and the discharge point to attenuate fan noise. In a cinema venue environment, noise bleed from the kitchen extraction is a real concern — the silencer addresses this at source.

Ductwork & Distribution

  • Fabrication and installation of approximately 16 metres of ductwork from canopy to discharge All ductwork fabricated in-house to the route confirmed on site. Includes all bends, transitions, and sections to complete the run. Exact length confirmed on installation day — the 16 metres is the surveyed estimate.
  • Access panels installed at 2-metre intervals throughout the full ductwork run A mandatory TR19 requirement. Access panels must be present throughout the duct run for inspection and cleaning. Without them, the system cannot receive a TR19 certificate and your insurance may be at risk. Approximately 8 panels across this run.
  • Rubber flexible connections at canopy spigot and fan inlet/outlet Rubber flex joints isolate vibration from the fan motor and prevent it transmitting through the ductwork into the building structure. Also allows minor positional tolerance during installation.

Electrical & Ancillaries

  • Circuit breaker — supply and installation Dedicated circuit breaker for the extraction fan, supplied and installed as part of this contract. Connection to the incoming electrical supply to be confirmed on site — any works to the incoming supply itself are by others.
  • Full fitting kit — all fixings, brackets, hangers, supports, and ancillaries Everything needed to secure the canopy, ductwork, and fan properly in position is included in the price. No day-one conversations about missing materials.

Commissioning & Handover

  • Full system commissioning and airflow balancing Once installation is complete, we run the system up, verify extraction performance at the canopy face, and set the inverter to the correct operating speed. The system is left working — not just installed.
  • As-fitted drawings supplied on completion A record of the installed system — duct route, fan position, access panel locations. Required for TR19 cleaning records and any future maintenance or inspection work.
  • Product data sheets and client walkthrough on handover Technical data sheets for all installed equipment provided on the day. We also walk you through the system before leaving — how to operate it, what to check, and what your TR19 cleaning obligations will be going forward.
Exclusions

Building works and structural penetrations beyond those described. Planning permission (Fan Rescue can advise). Gas supply works. Fire suppression. Electrical connection to the incoming supply (by others). Any work not explicitly listed above.

Ongoing Maintenance

Your System Will Need Regular TR19 Cleaning

Once installed, your extraction system must be cleaned at TR19-compliant intervals to remain EHO-compliant and keep your insurance valid. Grease build-up in uncleaned ductwork is a fire risk and a compliance failure.

Fan Rescue runs TR19 cleaning contracts across London. We already know your system — let us maintain it. We'll recommend the right cleaning frequency once we've seen how the kitchen runs in operation.
