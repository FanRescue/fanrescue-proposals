Proposed Scope of Works

What's Included

A complete basement mechanical ventilation and comfort cooling package — from the repositioning of the existing AHU through to commissioning of all new systems.

1. Relocation of Existing AHU Unit

  • Careful disconnection and relocation of existing AHU to new agreed position The existing Air Handling Unit will be safely isolated, disconnected from all services, and repositioned within the basement to the new agreed location. All works carried out with care to protect the unit and surrounding finishes.
  • Galvanised steel ductwork modifications to suit new layout Supply and installation of all necessary ductwork alterations required to connect the relocated AHU to the existing distribution system — fabricated and installed to BESA DW144 standards.
  • Anti-vibration mounts, rubber isolators, and flexible connections All AV mounts, isolators, and flexible duct connections fitted to the relocated unit to minimise noise transmission and vibration transfer to the building structure.
  • Reconnection to existing services and recommissioning Full reconnection to electrical supply and ductwork services. System tested and recommissioned to confirm full operational performance in its new position before any further works commence.

2. New Daikin AHU — Basement Ventilation System

  • Supply and installation of 1no Daikin Air Handling Unit A new Daikin AHU sized to serve the basement ventilation requirements, selected to deliver the required air change rates for the space. Daikin specified for reliability, parts availability, and energy efficiency.
  • Complete supply and extract ductwork system Full galvanised steel supply and extract ductwork installation, sized and routed to suit the basement layout and structural constraints. Installed in accordance with BESA DW144 guidance throughout.
  • Air distribution diffusers for balanced airflow Supply and installation of air distribution diffusers at all designated supply and extract points, selected and positioned to deliver even airflow distribution across all areas without draughts or dead zones.
  • Volume Control Dampers (VCDs) for airflow regulation and zoning VCDs installed at branch points throughout the ductwork system, allowing independent control of airflow to different zones and providing the means for accurate system balancing on commissioning.
  • Access panels at regular intervals to DW144 guidance Inspection and cleaning access panels installed throughout the ductwork installation at the intervals required by DW144. Essential for future maintenance and TR19 compliance.
  • Full system balancing to optimise air change rates On completion, all airflows measured, adjusted, and balanced across the distribution network to confirm each zone receives the correct supply and extract volumes for the intended use.

3. Dedicated Toilet Extraction System

  • High-performance inline extraction fan, sized for application Supply and installation of a standalone inline extract fan selected to deliver the ventilation rates required by Building Regulations for toilet areas, independent of the main AHU system.
  • Acoustic silencer to reduce breakout noise An inline acoustic attenuator installed on the extract ductwork to reduce fan noise breakout into adjacent occupied areas — critical for a professional office environment.
  • Full ductwork installation from extract points to termination Complete galvanised steel ductwork from the toilet extract valves through to the final discharge point, installed to DW144 and fully sealed to prevent odour migration.
  • Extract air valves within toilet areas Flush-fitting extract air valves installed at all extract positions within the toilet areas, adjustable for commissioning to deliver the correct extract rate at each point.

4. Air Conditioning System — Comfort Cooling

  • Supply and installation of 4no Daikin wall-mounted split units (2.5kW each) Four Daikin wall-mounted indoor units installed at agreed positions throughout the basement areas, each rated at 2.5kW cooling capacity. Daikin specified for proven reliability, quiet operation, and long-term parts support.
  • 1no outdoor condenser unit Supply and installation of a single outdoor condenser unit positioned at the agreed external location, serving all four indoor units. All necessary brackets, supports, and fixings included.
  • Full refrigeration pipework system — insulated and pressure tested Complete refrigerant pipework installation from outdoor unit to all four indoor units, fully insulated to prevent condensation and energy loss, and pressure tested prior to charging in accordance with F-Gas regulations.
  • Condensate drainage pipework with appropriate falls Condensate drain pipework installed from all indoor units to a suitable discharge point, run with the falls required to ensure gravity drainage and prevent blockages or overflow.
  • Electrical isolators and local connections Isolation switches and all local electrical connections to each indoor and the outdoor unit included. Main supply by others; Fan Rescue connects from the isolation point.
  • Anti-vibration mounts for outdoor unit Anti-vibration mounts installed beneath the outdoor condenser unit to minimise structural vibration and noise transmission — important given the occupied nature of the building above.

5. Commissioning & Handover

  • Full system commissioning, airflow balancing, and controls configuration All systems — AHU, toilet extract, and AC — commissioned on completion. Airflows measured, balanced, and set. AC controls and thermostats configured. Everything left working, not just installed.
  • As-fitted drawings supplied on completion A full record of the installed systems — duct routes, fan positions, access panel locations, and AC pipework routes. Required for future maintenance and any TR19 cleaning compliance records.
  • Product data sheets and client walkthrough on handover Technical data sheets for all installed equipment issued on the day. We'll walk Alasdair through each system — how to operate it, filter change schedules, and future maintenance requirements — before we leave site.
Exclusions

Main electrical supply and distribution cabling to isolation points (by others). Building works, core drilling, and structural penetrations beyond agreed service routes. Planning permission or listed building consents if required. Fire suppression systems. Any works not listed above.
