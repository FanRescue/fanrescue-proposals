The Challenge

Why Extraction Cleaning Can't Be an Afterthought

Grease accumulation in ductwork is a compliance, insurance, and fire risk. Most operators don't know there's a problem until an EHO visit or a claim is rejected.

🔥 Fire Risk

Grease buildup in ductwork is the leading cause of commercial kitchen fires. A single blockage can ignite from cooking temperatures.

📋 Compliance Gaps

EHOs and insurers require documented proof of regular cleaning. Without TR19 certificates, your insurance cover could be void.

⏰ Operational Disruption

Poor scheduling means cleaning happens at the wrong time — or not at all. You need a provider that works around your service, not against it.

📈 Rising Standards

BSI 15780 and TR/19 grease micron testing are now the benchmark. Operators who don't meet them risk enforcement action.
