Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of what we'll be supplying, installing, and commissioning at Battersea Bloom. The system is grouped into four areas: air handling and ventilation, ductwork and distribution, fire suppression, and commissioning and handover.

Air Handling & Ventilation

  • Air handling unit serving the toilet area A dedicated AHU providing extract ventilation to the WC areas, sized to comply with Building Regs Part F minimum extract rates. Continuous operation with appropriate timing controls so the toilets are properly ventilated during opening hours.
  • Air handling unit serving the customer seating area A larger AHU providing balanced supply and extract ventilation to the dining/customer space, sized to suit the room volume and occupancy. Keeps the seating area fresh, comfortable, and free of cooking odour carry-over.
  • Kitchen extraction canopy Stainless-steel wall-mounted extraction canopy positioned over the cooking line. Designed to capture grease, smoke, steam, and combustion gases at source. Supplied with grease filters and a lighting strip. Sized to the cooking equipment beneath.
  • Extract fan and inline silencers A dedicated extract fan handling the kitchen canopy duty, paired with rectangular silencers on both intake and discharge sides to bring in-room and external sound levels within acceptable limits. Speed-controlled to balance against the canopy face velocity.
  • Odour control system A carbon-stage odour control unit installed downstream of the extract fan to neutralise cooking odours before discharge. Critical in mixed-use developments where neighbouring residential or retail tenants share envelope space. Filters are serviceable and replaceable on a planned interval.
  • Passive fresh air supply via plenum boxes on existing grille A passive supply arrangement drawing fresh air through the existing external grille, distributed into the kitchen via plenum boxes. Sized to balance against the extract rate so the kitchen stays neutral or slightly negative — preventing cooking odours migrating into the customer area.
  • 3-phase electric battery heater on fresh air supply An in-duct electric battery (3-phase) tempers the incoming fresh air during cold months, stopping cold-air dump in the kitchen and keeping back-of-house staff comfortable. Thermostatic control with high-limit safety cut-out. Sized to the supply airflow.

Ductwork & Distribution

  • Standard galvanised steel ductwork — not fire rated All extract, supply, and fresh-air duct runs in galvanised mild steel, fabricated and installed to BESA DW 144 standard. Standard rectangular and circular sections — not fire-rated, on the basis that the route does not require fire-rated ductwork. To be confirmed on site survey against the building's fire strategy.
  • Diffusers, grilles, and discharge termination Supply diffusers and extract grilles selected to suit each space — kitchen, WCs, and customer area. Extract discharge routed to the agreed termination point with weatherproof cowl and anti-backdraft protection. Termination location confirmed on site survey.
  • Full fitting kit — supports, fixings, flexible connectors Everything needed to install the system properly: duct supports and brackets, anti-vibration mounts on fan units and AHUs, flexible connections to prevent noise and vibration transmission, and all associated fixings.

Fire Suppression — Ansul System

  • Ansul R-102 wet-chemical fire suppression system An Ansul R-102 fire suppression system installed under the kitchen extraction canopy, designed to detect and suppress cooking-equipment fires automatically. Wet-chemical agent specifically formulated for the high temperatures and grease loads of commercial cooking. Industry standard for insurance compliance and code requirements in commercial kitchens.
  • Detection, nozzle layout, and gas/electric shut-off interlock Heat-detection links positioned over each cooking appliance, suppression nozzles aimed per the Ansul design, and a manual pull station at the kitchen exit. The system interlocks with the gas/electric supply so that on activation, the energy source to the cooking line is automatically shut off.
  • Commissioning and certification System commissioned and certified by an Ansul-trained engineer. Certification documentation issued on handover for your insurance and building-control records. Annual service interval recommended thereafter.

Commissioning & Handover

  • Full system commissioning and airflow balancing Once installation is complete, we run all systems up, verify supply and extract performance against design, and balance airflows so the kitchen, WCs, and customer area are all properly ventilated and pressure-balanced. Battery heater and odour control verified through their full operating range.
  • BESA-compliant installation documentation Installation carried out under our BESA certification (HV020676) to DW 144 / DW 172 standards. Commissioning certificates issued for the ventilation system and the Ansul system on handover.
  • Product data sheets and client walkthrough on handover Technical data sheets for all installed equipment provided on the day. We walk you through the systems — how to operate them, how to change filters, what to check day to day, and the recommended service schedule.
