What's Included

Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of everything being supplied, installed, and commissioned at Test Restaurant. This is a complete system — nothing is left out of the price unless stated under Exclusions.

Extraction System

  • Supply and installation of 1500 x 1000mm stainless steel extraction canopy Sized to cover the cooking line. 304-grade stainless steel with integrated grease baffles as standard.
  • Supply and installation of centrifugal extraction fan with inverter drive Variable speed control for energy efficiency. Turn down during quieter periods, up during full service.

Ductwork & Distribution

  • Fabrication and installation of approximately 12 metres of galvanised steel ductwork All ductwork fabricated in-house. Includes bends, transitions, and sections to complete the run from canopy to discharge.
  • TR19-compliant access panels at 2-metre intervals Mandatory for inspection and cleaning. Without these, the system cannot receive a TR19 certificate.

Commissioning & Handover

  • Full system commissioning and airflow balancing System tested, extraction performance verified, inverter configured. Left working — not just installed.
  • As-fitted drawings and documentation Duct route, fan position, access panel locations documented. Required for TR19 records and future maintenance.
