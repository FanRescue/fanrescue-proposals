M&E Design Pack — Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of the design package being produced for Olle at Battersea Power Station. This covers everything needed to progress the kitchen extraction and ventilation project from concept to costed installation proposal.

Design Drawings

  • Proposed M&E kitchen extraction and ventilation design drawings Full layout drawings showing the proposed extraction system, supply air routes, fan positions, ductwork runs, and canopy locations — tailored to your Korean BBQ kitchen layout and cooking equipment.

Technical Reporting

  • Technical installation report A detailed engineering report covering system design rationale, airflow calculations, equipment selections, and installation methodology. This document supports landlord and planning submissions.
  • Odour impact assessment An assessment of the odour discharge from the proposed extraction system, including dispersion modelling where required. Korean BBQ generates significant cooking odours — this report demonstrates how the system manages that for the local authority and landlord.

Plant Selection & Specifications

  • Plant selection and equipment specifications Detailed specifications for all proposed plant — fans, filters, canopies, odour control units, and ancillaries. Each item selected for the specific demands of the site and the Korean BBQ cooking style.

Installation Quotation

  • Detailed quotation for the proposed installation works A fully costed proposal to supply and install the designed system — labour, materials, commissioning, and handover. This gives you a firm price to proceed with the build, with no gaps between design and delivery.
Exclusions

Physical installation works (quoted separately within the design pack). Structural surveys or calculations by others. Planning application fees. Any third-party reports not listed above.

Ongoing Maintenance

Your System Will Need Regular TR19 Cleaning

Once installed, your extraction system must be cleaned at TR19-compliant intervals to remain EHO-compliant and keep your insurance valid. Grease build-up in uncleaned ductwork is a fire risk and a compliance failure.

Fan Rescue runs TR19 cleaning contracts across London. We already know your system — let us maintain it. We'll recommend the right cleaning frequency once we've seen how the kitchen runs in operation.
