Why It Matters

TR19 Compliance — The Three Reasons

Kitchen extraction cleaning isn't just housekeeping. It's a fire safety, insurance, and EHO obligation rolled into one.

Fire Safety

Grease accumulation in ductwork is the leading cause of commercial kitchen extraction fires. Charcoal grills add carbon deposits and higher discharge temperatures, which is why those kitchens are cleaned more often.

Insurance Validity

Most commercial insurance policies require evidence of TR19-compliant cleaning at the recommended frequency. Without a current certificate, a fire claim can be denied outright.

EHO & Landlord Requirements

Environmental Health Officers and most landlords now ask to see a current TR19 certificate during inspections. Non-compliance can affect your hygiene rating and lease standing.

What's Included

Scope of Each Clean

The same scope applies at every site on every visit. All work is carried out to TR19® Grease (BESA).

Extraction System Cleaning

  • Canopy cleaned internally and externally, including plenum and grease troughs
  • Grease filters removed, degreased and refitted
  • Extract ductwork cleaned to the full extent accessible through existing access panels
  • Extract fan impeller and housing cleaned; discharge inspected
  • Work carried out outside trading hours, at times agreed with each branch

Documentation & Certification

  • Wet film thickness (WFTT) grease deposit readings before and after cleaning, to TR19® Grease
  • Before and after photographs throughout the system
  • Post-clean report, system schematic and certificate of cleanliness within 5 working days of each clean
  • Recommended cleaning frequency stated on each certificate
  • Any inaccessible sections or remedial items flagged in writing
Access Panels

Prices cover cleaning through the access panels already installed. Where we find sections of ductwork that cannot be reached, we will record them in the report and quote separately for additional TR19-compliant access panels. Panels are only fitted with your written approval.

Not Included

Each of the following will be quoted separately if required.

  • Repair or replacement of fans, motors, filters, ductwork or canopies
  • Builder's work and making good
  • Scaffolding or powered access to roof-level plant
  • Parking and congestion charges
  • Cleaning of supply / fresh-air systems

Recommended Schedule

Cleaning Frequency Guide

Frequencies follow TR19® Grease (BESA), the specification insurers and fire authorities refer to.

Cooking Type Recommended Frequency
Heavy use (chargrills, woks, deep fat fryers) Every 3 months
Moderate use (general cooking, ovens, mixed menus) Every 6 months
Light use (reheating, sandwich prep) Every 12 months

Our recommendation for Maroush Group: Charcoal and solid-fuel cooking produces heavy grease and carbon deposits and carries a higher fire risk, and the restaurants trade long hours — up to 18 hours a day. All six restaurants are therefore recommended for quarterly cleaning. The Park Royal CPU uses gas burners for general cooking only and is recommended for six-monthly cleaning.

We will review each frequency after the first clean, using the grease deposit readings taken. Each certificate will state the recommended frequency.
