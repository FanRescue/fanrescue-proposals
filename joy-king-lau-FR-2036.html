Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of the cleaning service proposed for your extraction system at Joy King Lau Restaurant, 3 Leicester Street, London WC2H 7BL. All work is carried out in accordance with TR19 guidelines and current fire safety regulations.

Extraction System Cleaning
  • Full internal clean of all extraction ductwork from canopy to discharge point — complete degreasing of the full duct run using industry-approved methods and chemicals, all accessible sections cleaned to TR19 standard
  • Canopy deep clean and degreasing — full strip-down and degreasing of canopy internals including baffles, plenums, and grease channels, external surfaces cleaned and polished
  • Fan unit strip-down, clean, and reassembly — fan impeller, casing, and motor housing fully degreased, checked for wear, reassembled, and tested on completion
  • Access panel inspection and cleaning of all accessible sections — all existing access panels opened, inspected, and cleaned, any inaccessible areas noted in the report
Documentation & Certification
  • Full photographic report with before and after images — detailed photographic evidence of the system condition before and after cleaning, provided digitally within 48 hours of payment
  • TR19 compliance certificate — issued upon receipt of payment, confirms your extraction system has been cleaned to TR19 standard as required by your EHO and insurer
